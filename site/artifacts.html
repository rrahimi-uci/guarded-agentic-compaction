Artifact provenance

The Pages workflow copies the tracked paper PDF and technical deck into its download area, materializes the benchmark explorer from its checked-in source, and copies the authored site pages. The article page carries a digest of paper/tex/; the Pages builder refuses deployment when that digest, the generated benchmark explorer, or the generated quantitative method page drifts.

Evidence boundary: the headline efficiency results are research-prototype measurements on pinned public GitHub records and live provider calls. They do not establish production certification, semantic equivalence, full-workflow cross-repository or time-forward generalization, or superiority to hand-written code. A narrower two-read extension does carry cross-repository, time-forward evidence; see the research page for its separate claim boundary.
