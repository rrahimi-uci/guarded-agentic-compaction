\section{Conclusion}

\method treats recurrence as candidate evidence, not permission to rewrite an
agent: it preserves exact held-out contracts while saving resources, retires all
families on three external substrates, and on the fourth admits a small region,
retires four more at the margin, and is structurally eligible only under some agent
architectures. Judge workflow optimizers by their evidence, refusals, and
fallback, not only by the turns they remove.
