\section{Conclusion}

\method treats recurrent traces as candidate evidence, not permission to rewrite
an agent. Across three live GitHub workflow families and a cross-repository
extension, compile-or-retire yields substantial resource savings while
preserving exact held-out contracts; on NESTFUL and API-Bank it refuses
entirely. Judge workflow optimizers not only by the turns they remove, but by
the evidence they require, what they refuse, and the baseline they fall back
to.
