\section{Conclusion}

\method treats recurrence as candidate evidence, not permission to rewrite an
agent: it preserves exact held-out contracts while saving resources, refuses on
three external substrates, and on the one that permits admission admits a small
region, refuses four more at the margin, and dispatches only under some agent
architectures. Judge workflow optimizers by their evidence, refusals, and
fallback, not only by the turns they remove.
