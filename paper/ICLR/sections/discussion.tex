\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}

The strongest supported claim is narrow: repeated read-only workflow prefixes
can become guarded deterministic programs that preserve exact held-out
contracts on real GitHub families while failing closed when evidence is
inadequate, including on NESTFUL, API-Bank, and one cross-repository cohort.

\paragraph{Gate maturity is the main scientific risk.}
\Cref{prop:admission} is per-candidate, not compiler-wide over the adaptive
search of \cref{alg:compile}; certification requires freezing the candidate
relative to calibration, and a Bonferroni repair over $m$ candidates would use
$\gamma=\delta/(m|\Lambda|)$, raising the zero-violation requirement from 92
admitted groups to 106 at $m=2$. The gate is also empirically step-like at
$n_\eta\ge 92$: we show that it \emph{refuses} correctly far better than that
$q$ \emph{discriminates}. Refitting $q$ for a genuine frontier is the key
follow-up.

\paragraph{Group independence is assumed, not established.}
\Cref{prop:admission} needs i.i.d.\ groups. The live studies set
\code{min\_days}\,=\,1 and \code{min\_principals}\,=\,1 over one repository
snapshot, so a ``group'' is one record from one project at one instant, sharing a
schema, an author community, and a period with its neighbours. The grouped split
makes exchangeability plausible; nothing in the calibration verifies it.

\paragraph{External validity and practical competitiveness.}
The richer evidence comes from one revision-pinned repository and one
provider/model family, and the cross-repository extension is a narrower,
template-tied task. We establish no superiority under drift or across providers,
and do not price manual authoring or maintenance.

\paragraph{Semantic scope, and where ``unchanged baseline'' is exact.}
Exact task contracts do not certify semantic equivalence once a model
continuation follows; retained-continuation replay exposes that gap. Writes,
handoffs, hosted tools, undeclared effects, and guardrail evaluations at a
removed turn lie outside \cref{prop:admission}. The fallback claim is scoped too:
every check that rejects \emph{before} emission is exact for both integration
paths, but only a staging-owning runner can restore byte-identical model input,
since an adapter cannot withdraw a response the host already committed
(\cref{app:algorithms}).
