\section{Conclusion}

\method discovers grounded read-only prefixes with explicit admission and
refusal conditions, reduces model work on recurrent GitHub workflows and
schema-first BIRD openings, and refuses where support is missing. Its artifacts
carry replay certificates and, for one post-cutoff cohort, an end-to-end one. We
do not claim graded learned selection, completeness-check benefit,
unfiltered-deployment bounds, or superior quality--cost over simpler compilation.
