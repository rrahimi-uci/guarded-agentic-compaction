\section{Conclusion}

\method treats recurrence as candidate evidence, not permission to rewrite an
agent: it preserves exact held-out contracts while saving resources, re-derives
the same artifacts on a second model and a second provider, retires all families
on three external substrates, and admits a small, architecture-limited region on
the fourth. Judge workflow optimizers by their evidence, refusals, and
fallback, not only by the turns they remove.
