\section{Conclusion}

\method automates the program an engineer would write: it compiles and admits grounded
read-only prefixes under a finite-sample bound, refuses elsewhere, and on GitHub
matches hand-written programs, cutting cost 58.7\% (primary families) and 73.6\%
(eleven repositories). Replay certificates cover selected populations,
two primary families miss the adjusted target, and end-to-end bounds cover one
family; we claim no learned selection or advantage over simpler compilation.
