\section{Conclusion}

\method treats recurrence as candidate evidence, not permission to rewrite an
agent: it preserves exact held-out contracts while saving resources within one
snapshot, certifies the end-to-end contract on one fresh cohort, re-derives two
of three programs on a second model and provider, retires three external
substrates and admits a small region on the fourth, and showed no measured
guard value under drift. Judge such optimizers by evidence, refusals, and
fallback, not by turns removed.
