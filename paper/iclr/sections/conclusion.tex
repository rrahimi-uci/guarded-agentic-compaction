\section{Conclusion}

\method treats recurrence as evidence, not permission: it saves resources while
showing no detected accuracy loss in the primary GitHub and BIRD comparisons,
certifies the
end-to-end contract on one fresh cohort, refuses where evidence is thin, and
shows request-scope protection on constructed live tasks. Completeness
checking can duplicate the agent's own repair; earlier drift tests remain
negative. The evidence supports bounded compilation and mechanism-specific
safeguards, not universal quality preservation or learned-gate superiority.
