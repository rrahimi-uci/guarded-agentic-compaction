\section{Conclusion}

\method compiles grounded read-only prefixes only under explicit admission and
refusal conditions, cuts model work on recurrent GitHub workflows and
schema-first BIRD openings, and refuses where support is missing. Its replay
certificates cover selected populations, two primary families miss the
selection-adjusted 5\% target, and end-to-end bounds cover one task family.
We claim no graded learned selection, completeness-check benefit, or
quality--cost advantage over simpler compilation.
