\section{Conclusion}

\method treats recurrence as candidate evidence, not permission to rewrite an
agent: it preserves held-out contracts while saving resources within one
snapshot, certifies the end-to-end contract on one fresh cohort, compiles a live
SQL agent's opening on a public benchmark, refuses where evidence is thin, and
showed no measured guard value under drift. Judge such optimizers by evidence,
refusals, and fallback, not by turns removed.
