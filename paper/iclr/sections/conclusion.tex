\section{Conclusion}

\method automates the program an engineer would write: it compiles grounded
read-only prefixes, admits them under a finite-sample bound, refuses elsewhere,
and on GitHub workflows matches hand-written programs at about 73\% lower model
cost than the unchanged agent. Replay certificates cover selected populations,
two primary families miss the adjusted target, and end-to-end bounds cover one
family; we claim no learned selection or advantage over simpler compilation.
