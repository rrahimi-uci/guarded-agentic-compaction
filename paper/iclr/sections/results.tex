Across the 90 held-out records, \method satisfies 90/90 exact contracts and the
unchanged agent 89/90 (\cref{tab:primary}), reducing provider requests by
66.6\%, tokens by 63.1\%, latency by 64.2\%, and cost by 58.7\% (per-record
values in \cref{tab:absolute-resources}, \cref{app:stats}). Issue-type routing admits
only a two-read prefix; the two deeper workflows approach 75\% fewer requests.

The discordant pair is one baseline-only miss (backlog record 5189, an
ungrounded excerpt) against zero compiled-only misses (McNemar $p=1$; one-sided
95\% upper bound on compiled-only discordance 3.3\%, or 2.2\% over 210 records
with the extension in \cref{app:stats});
the same 30 records rerun on 2026-08-18 scored 30/30 in every arm
(\cref{app:limits}), so the miss is a stochastic baseline event and the result
supports preservation on this cohort, not superiority or equivalence. Each artifact was
admitted with 92 zero-violation calibration groups
($U_{\hat\eta}=0.0498\le0.05$), a bound conditional on i.i.d.\ groups. The
certificate is compiler-wide for issue-type routing, where one candidate
reached calibration, and per-candidate for PR-outcome and backlog routing,
where two did; correcting for both gives $U=0.0569$, above $\alpha$, so those
two are certified compiler-wide only at $\alpha\ge0.057$ (\cref{cor:frozen},
\cref{tab:multiplicity-accounting} in \cref{app:config}). On \code{gpt-6-luna} and on
Anthropic \code{claude-sonnet-5}, same cohorts, re-discovery from each model's
own traces re-derives two of the three artifacts and refuses the third; the
transfer of the retained artifacts to \code{gpt-6-luna} keeps the structure
with one compiled-only miss in 90 (\cref{app:limits}).

Discovery reaches the manual ceiling: programs written with full knowledge of
the workflow also reach 90/90 and, priced on the same axes
(\cref{tab:manual-resources}), tie on requests, issue fewer interfaces, are
marginally cheaper in tokens and dollars, and lose only on latency. We claim
discovery and admission, not runtime dominance over hand-written code.

\subsection{Time-forward transfer: compile or retire}
\label{sec:res-multirepo}

On the narrower two-read pull-request outcome task across frozen public
repositories (\cref{sec:setup}), \method completes 120/120 exact
pairs on four of five repositories and retires \code{pytorch/pytorch} at
admission, where no calibration group is accepted at any threshold, rather
than loosening its gate (\cref{app:breadth}). The compiled program ran on 80/120
held-out records: every open-class record abstained on the induced verifier's
\code{pr.state} hull, learned from discovery pools holding 0--3 open records,
and ran the unchanged agent, so requests fall 44.4\% overall (66.7\% on
dispatched records) and tokens 52.4\%. A balanced three-repository rerun, with all three
classes in discovery, dispatches on 180/180 and reaches 180/180 exact pairs at 66.7\% fewer requests and 78.4\% fewer tokens
(\cref{tab:multirepo-disaggregated} in \cref{app:breadth}). A fixed two-read
template is more efficient in the core protocol and tied in the rerun.

The external substrates isolate the binding constraint (\cref{tab:funnel}). All
four clear provenance, synthesis, and replay with \emph{no} wrong execution,
then NESTFUL, API-Bank, and executed BFCL gold plans retire, each settled
arithmetically before the compiler ran. AppWorld's gold solutions reach $n_{\max}=136$, making it the first
external corpus where \cref{eq:cp} is satisfiable, and it admits one artifact at
92/92 zero-violation groups ($U_{\hat\eta}=0.0498$) with 11/11 exact replay on
its sealed admission split, under both pre-registered effect arms. Two features
bound it: the gate is step-like and the admitted program is argument-free
(\cref{app:external}).

Admissibility is not dispatchability. Gold solutions are not agent trajectories,
so we also test the admitted region against AppWorld's 28 released baseline
runs. Over 8{,}190 trajectories it is structurally eligible at the entry boundary in
$37.9\%$, and the architecture explains the spread (\cref{tab:appworld-dispatch} in \cref{app:external}):
full-code agents are eligible almost always, ReAct and plan-and-execute almost
never. They open by reading API documentation, yet admitting that read as a
prologue recovers only 2 of 4{,}680, because they rarely execute the region at
all (\cref{app:limits}); the measurement bounds $\varphi$ from above.
