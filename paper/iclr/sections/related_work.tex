\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Trace compilation and specialization.}
\method combines trace specialization~\citep{bolz2009pypy}, partial
evaluation~\citep{futamura1971partial,jones1993partial}, effect
declarations~\citep{lucassen1988effects}, process mining~\citep{vanderAalst2016process},
and bounded synthesis~\citep{gulwani2011flashfill,solarlezama2006sketching}.
Its induced verifiers inherit the limitation of likely
invariants~\citep{ernst2001daikon}: an observed hull is evidence, not proof.

\paragraph{Reuse, caching, and optimization of agents.}
TraceCompiler~\citep{elyadouni2026tracecompiler} also mines agent traces into
mostly deterministic workflows, uses argument-level producer evidence, and
refuses underdetermined behavior. \method studies a narrower execution unit:
initial read-only prefixes synthesized in a bounded DSL, with effect and
position barriers and a fixed-candidate finite-sample admission rule.
This composition and its measured limits distinguish the work; trace-to-program
reuse and provenance are not unique to \method. No matched execution comparison
with TraceCompiler is reported.
AWM induces reusable workflows that guide subsequent model generations
\citep{wang2025awm}; GAC executes grounded prefixes without those decisions.
Plan caching reuses plans \citep{zhang2025plancache}; EvoC2F compiles an
annotated orchestration IR and tests skills before admission \citep{wei2026evoc2f};
Agent JIT generates and schedules executable plans with tool pre/postconditions
\citep{winston2026agentjit}. These are distinct optimization units, not
interchangeable implementations. Other systems optimize workflows, compile
plans, or prune topology
\citep{abuzakuk2026awo,li2026flowcompile,chen2026agentslimming,wang2026covenant,chundru2026agenticcompilation};
skill libraries grow reusable procedures from
experience~\citep{wang2023voyager,zheng2025skillweaver}; semantic caches reuse
responses~\citep{bang2023gptcache}, and prompt caching and cache-aware
compression reuse shared prefixes~\citep{lumer2026cache,song2026capc};
DSPy, GEPA, TextGrad, and Trace optimize prompts and programs against task
feedback~\citep{khattab2024dspy,opsahlong2024mipro,agrawal2026gepa,yuksekgonul2024textgrad,cheng2024trace};
RouteLLM changes models~\citep{ong2025routellm}, LLMCompiler parallelizes
calls~\citep{kim2024llmcompiler}, and \citet{yue2026workflowsurvey} survey the
space. Our contribution combines trace reuse with a finite-sample admission bound
and exact fallback on clean rejection; the reviewed systems motivate its
components rather than establishing a universal priority claim. Counterfactual trace auditing~\citep{zhou2026cta}, which
pairs each trace with a counterfactual run of the same task, is the closest
analogue of \method's continuation check.

\paragraph{Selective prediction and finite-sample risk control.}
Dispatch-or-fall-back is selective prediction with a reject
option~\citep{chow1970reject,geifman2017selective}. The admission bound is a
fixed-grid instance of learn-then-test~\citep{angelopoulos2022ltt} using the
exact binomial interval~\citep{clopper1934binomial}; the risk-controlling
prediction-set framework gives related high-probability risk guarantees
\citep{bates2021rcps}; conformal prediction and conformal risk control give
marginal-coverage and expected-loss guarantees, which
differ~\citep{vovk2005algorithmic,angelopoulos2024crc}.

\paragraph{Tool-use safety and evaluation.}
ToolEmu and AgentDojo motivate adversarial tool evaluation
\citep{ruan2024toolemu,debenedetti2024agentdojo}. Tool-use benchmarks
\citep{patil2025bfcl,qin2023toolllm,basu2025nestful,li2023apibank,trivedi2024appworld,lu2025toolsandbox,barres2025tau2}
measure broader capabilities; \method measures live BIRD efficiency and four
compiler substrates (\cref{app:external}).
