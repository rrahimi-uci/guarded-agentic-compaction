\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Trace compilation and specialization.}
\method adapts the tracing-JIT pattern---record a hot path, compile it behind
a guard, deoptimize on a miss~\citep{bala2000dynamo,gal2009tracejit,holzle1992deopt}---and
meta-tracing's observation that the trace, not the program, is the unit of
specialization~\citep{bolz2009pypy}. Partial evaluation supplies the older
frame: a program specialized to known inputs is correct only where the static
assumptions hold~\citep{futamura1971partial,jones1993partial}. The analogues
here are grounded arguments (the static input), declared effects
\citep{lucassen1988effects}, and the manifest pins that play the role of the
guard; the novelty is not the inequality but that each of these can veto.

\paragraph{Mining workflows from traces.}
Family mining is a narrow form of process mining, which recovers models from
event logs~\citep{vanderAalst2016process}, and synthesis is bounded
programming by example and sketching
\citep{gulwani2011flashfill,solarlezama2006sketching}, with provenance tracked
explicitly~\citep{cheney2009provenance}. Likely invariants inferred from
executions~\citep{ernst2001daikon} are the ancestor of the induced
verifier, and \cref{app:limits} shows the same caveat applies: an inferred
hull is evidence, not a proof.

\paragraph{Reuse, caching, and optimization of agents.}
Agent systems mine workflows, cache plans, compile orchestration, validate
plans, or prune topology
\citep{wang2025awm,zhang2025plancache,abuzakuk2026awo,wei2026evoc2f,winston2026agentjit,li2026flowcompile,chen2026agentslimming,wang2026covenant,chundru2026agenticcompilation};
skill libraries grow reusable procedures from
experience~\citep{wang2023voyager,zheng2025skillweaver}; semantic and prompt
caches reuse responses~\citep{bang2023gptcache,lumer2026cache,song2026capc};
DSPy, GEPA, TextGrad, and Trace optimize prompts and programs against task
feedback~\citep{khattab2024dspy,agrawal2026gepa,yuksekgonul2024textgrad,cheng2024trace};
RouteLLM changes models~\citep{ong2025routellm} and LLMCompiler parallelizes
calls~\citep{kim2024llmcompiler}; \citet{yue2026workflowsurvey} survey the
space. These systems decide \emph{what} to reuse; none states an admission
rule with a finite-sample guarantee or a fallback whose semantics are
exactly the unchanged agent. \method treats recurrence only as a proposal
(\cref{tab:mechanism-removal} in \cref{app:limits}), and counterfactual trace
auditing~\citep{zhou2026cta} is the instrument its continuation check
approximates.

\paragraph{Selective prediction and finite-sample risk control.}
Dispatch-or-fall-back is selective prediction with a reject
option~\citep{chow1970reject,geifman2017selective}. The admission bound is a
fixed-grid instance of learn-then-test~\citep{angelopoulos2022ltt} using the
exact binomial interval~\citep{clopper1934binomial}; the risk-controlling
prediction-set and conformal risk-control lineage
\citep{vovk2005algorithmic,bates2021rcps,angelopoulos2024crc} gives the
same kind of statement for other losses. The contribution here is not the
inequality but the composition that precedes it and the refusal it defaults
to.

\paragraph{Tool-use safety and evaluation.}
Effect declarations descend from effect systems~\citep{lucassen1988effects};
ToolEmu and AgentDojo evaluate agents against hazardous or injected tool
environments~\citep{ruan2024toolemu,debenedetti2024agentdojo}, which is the
regime the perturbation suite simulates provider-free (\cref{app:limits}).
BFCL, ToolLLM, NESTFUL, API-Bank, AppWorld, ToolSandbox, and $\tau^2$-bench
score tool use or interactive agents broadly
\citep{patil2025bfcl,qin2023toolllm,basu2025nestful,li2023apibank,trivedi2024appworld,lu2025toolsandbox,barres2025tau2};
\method uses four of them as compiler substrates and reports what each
refuses (\cref{app:external}).
