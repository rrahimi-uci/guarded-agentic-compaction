\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Trace compilation and specialization.}
Beyond the trace-compilation precedent of the introduction, \method takes
meta-tracing's unit of specialization, the trace~\citep{bolz2009pypy}.
Partial evaluation is the older frame: a specialized program is correct only
where its static assumptions hold~\citep{futamura1971partial,jones1993partial};
here grounded arguments, declared effects~\citep{lucassen1988effects}, and
manifest pins are the guard, and each can veto.

\paragraph{Mining workflows from traces.}
Family mining is a narrow form of process mining~\citep{vanderAalst2016process};
synthesis is bounded programming by example and sketching
\citep{gulwani2011flashfill,solarlezama2006sketching} with explicit
provenance~\citep{cheney2009provenance}; and likely invariants inferred from
executions~\citep{ernst2001daikon} are the ancestor of the induced verifier,
with the same caveat (\cref{app:limits}): an inferred hull is evidence, not proof.

\paragraph{Reuse, caching, and optimization of agents.}
Agent systems mine workflows, cache plans, compile orchestration, validate
plans, or prune topology
\citep{wang2025awm,zhang2025plancache,abuzakuk2026awo,wei2026evoc2f,winston2026agentjit,li2026flowcompile,chen2026agentslimming,wang2026covenant,chundru2026agenticcompilation};
skill libraries grow reusable procedures from
experience~\citep{wang2023voyager,zheng2025skillweaver}; semantic caches reuse
responses~\citep{bang2023gptcache}, and prompt caching and cache-aware
compression reuse shared prefixes~\citep{lumer2026cache,song2026capc};
DSPy, GEPA, TextGrad, and Trace optimize prompts and programs against task
feedback~\citep{khattab2024dspy,agrawal2026gepa,yuksekgonul2024textgrad,cheng2024trace};
RouteLLM changes models~\citep{ong2025routellm}, LLMCompiler parallelizes
calls~\citep{kim2024llmcompiler}, and \citet{yue2026workflowsurvey} survey the
space. These systems decide \emph{what} to reuse; to our knowledge none pairs
admission with a finite-sample bound and a fallback that is exactly the
unchanged agent. Counterfactual trace auditing~\citep{zhou2026cta}, which
pairs each trace with a counterfactual run of the same task, is the closest
analogue of \method's continuation check.

\paragraph{Selective prediction and finite-sample risk control.}
Dispatch-or-fall-back is selective prediction with a reject
option~\citep{chow1970reject,geifman2017selective}. The admission bound is a
fixed-grid instance of learn-then-test~\citep{angelopoulos2022ltt} using the
exact binomial interval~\citep{clopper1934binomial}; the risk-controlling
prediction-set and conformal risk-control lineage
\citep{vovk2005algorithmic,bates2021rcps,angelopoulos2024crc} gives the
same kind of statement for other losses.

\paragraph{Tool-use safety and evaluation.}
ToolEmu and AgentDojo evaluate agents against hazardous or injected tool
environments~\citep{ruan2024toolemu,debenedetti2024agentdojo}, the regime
our perturbation studies probe (\cref{app:limits}). BFCL, ToolLLM, NESTFUL,
API-Bank, AppWorld, ToolSandbox, and $\tau^2$-bench score tool use broadly
\citep{patil2025bfcl,qin2023toolllm,basu2025nestful,li2023apibank,trivedi2024appworld,lu2025toolsandbox,barres2025tau2};
\method runs a live agent on BIRD~\citep{li2023bird} and uses four of these as
compiler substrates, reporting what each refuses (\cref{app:external}).
