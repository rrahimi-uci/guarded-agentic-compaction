\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}

The supported claim is narrow: repeated read-only prefixes can become guarded
programs that preserve held-out contracts on the tested GitHub families and
retire when evidence is inadequate. \Cref{app:limits} expands each limitation.

\paragraph{Gate maturity is the main scientific risk.}
\Cref{prop:admission} is per-candidate, not compiler-wide over
\cref{alg:compile}'s adaptive search unless that search is frozen
(\cref{cor:frozen}); the primary families do not freeze, so a multiplicity
repair would raise their requirement to 106 groups. The gate is also step-like at $n_\eta\ge 92$, from two causes this design cannot
separate: every cohort fixes $|\mathcal{K}|=92$, so no intermediate coverage is
\emph{certifiable} whatever $q$ ranks (\cref{sec:method}), and $q$
\emph{refuses} correctly far better than it \emph{discriminates}. A pre-registered protocol (\cref{app:prospective}) has since run across five
repositories and 240 held-out triples, where a support-only comparator sharing
every mechanism but the risk budget is indistinguishable from the learned gate,
and neither shows graded coverage. The follow-up is to buy calibration groups
first: below $\lceil 92/c\rceil$, no refit of $q$ can be read.

\paragraph{Group independence is assumed, not established.}
\Cref{prop:admission} needs i.i.d.\ groups and neither corpus establishes
exchangeability: GitHub groups are single records from one snapshot with
\code{min\_days}=\code{min\_principals}=1, and AppWorld's task groups share a
scenario's supervisor and database. A seeded simulation (\cref{app:proof})
prices the exposure --- miscoverage inflates well past nominal under
block-correlated groups --- and the cluster-aware repairs all cost effective
sample size.

\paragraph{External validity, dispatchability, and comparison.}
The richer evidence comes from one revision-pinned repository and one
provider/model family, and admission is not dispatchability: \cref{eq:prefix} is
the operative limit, which \cref{sec:res-refusal} prices where ReAct and
plan-and-execute open with a documentation read past position~0. We show no
superiority under drift or across providers, do not price manual authoring, and
run no head-to-head against the workflow-reuse line, whose prompt-level
induction, parallel scheduling, and plan caching make no admissibility decision
to compare against --- leaving the \emph{unguarded} ablation unrun. Exact
contracts also do not certify a downstream continuation, and writes, handoffs,
hosted tools, undeclared effects, and removed-turn guardrails lie outside
\cref{prop:admission}.
