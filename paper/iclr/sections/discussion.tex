\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}

The supported claim is narrow: repeated read-only prefixes can become guarded
programs that preserve held-out contracts on the tested GitHub families and
retire when evidence is inadequate. Across 810 held-out episodes run under a compiled artifact on the
calibrated model in six live studies, one produced a compiled-only contract
failure, an excerpt-fidelity miss on identical evidence (\cref{app:stats});
\cref{app:limits} pairs limitations with protocols and \cref{app:claims} maps
claims to evidence.

\paragraph{Three constraints bound the claim.} First, admission control is
compiler-wide only when one candidate reaches calibration or the confidence
budget is divided across all that do. That holds for issue-type routing and for
every cross-repository family; PR-outcome and backlog routing calibrated two
candidates on 92 groups, where the corrected bound is 0.057
(\cref{app:config}). Second, with $|\mathcal{K}|=92$ the registered exact bound
certifies only full coverage, so no experiment here demonstrates graded
selectivity of $q$: the prospective protocol (\cref{app:prospective}) found the
learned gate and a support-only comparator indistinguishable across four
of five repositories and 240 held-out triples, and $q$ is constant within each
repository there (its entry state is one integer). Third, the binomial result is conditional on i.i.d.\ groups: the
92-record live calibration cohorts span 86--90 distinct creation days and 60--82 distinct authors per
family, so under the registered grid a bound that treats a day or an author as one
unit retires in every family (\cref{tab:effective-units} in \cref{app:limits};
\cref{app:config} gives a single-rule sensitivity, not a certificate), and AppWorld's task
groups share a scenario's supervisor and database (\cref{app:proof} prices the
exposure).

\paragraph{External validity, dispatchability, and comparison.}
The richer evidence comes from one pinned repository snapshot, extended by
five frozen cross-repository snapshots and one post-cutoff cohort of a single
family, and one calibrated model family; a second model family and a second
provider each re-derive two of the three artifacts from their own traces and
refuse the third, and every compiled-only miss observed is an excerpt-fidelity
error of the model (\cref{app:limits}). Under drift we show no superiority:
two provider-free perturbation studies are null and a live continuation-graded
study is adverse (an emptied list lies inside the learned hull, so both arms
answered wrongly on 39 of 60 records and the verifier prevented no silent wrong
answer; \cref{app:limits}). We do not price the human effort of manual
authoring and run no head-to-head against workflow-reuse systems;
\cref{tab:mechanism-removal} in \cref{app:limits} prices the guards internally,
and a recurrence-only replay on issue-type routing passed 30/30 at one request
per record, buying no measured quality (\cref{app:limits}). The registered
certificates cover the compiled region's replay, so the compiled-only misses
above lie outside their event; the end-to-end certificate of \cref{app:limits}
is per-candidate on one family. Writes, handoffs,
hosted tools, undeclared effects, and removed-turn guardrails lie outside
\cref{prop:admission}.
