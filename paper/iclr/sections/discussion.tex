\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}

Across 810 compiled held-out GitHub episodes in six studies on the calibrated
model, compiled-only failures are 1/270 on excerpt-bearing contracts
(one-sided bound 1.7\%) and 0/540 on outcome-only contracts (354 distinct
records; \cref{app:stats,app:claims}).

\paragraph{Certificate assumptions.} \Cref{tab:evidence-scope,tab:certificates}
separate replay from final-answer risk. The amended 184-group issue study
retired; a sequential extension selects $\eta=0.50$ on 311 pooled groups, but its
adaptive design and mixed frames make nominal admission no certificate, and
graded selection is not claimed (\cref{app:gate-extension}). Every registered
certificate assumes i.i.d.\ groups: counting a creation day or an author as one
unit retires every primary family under the registered grid
(\cref{tab:effective-units,app:config}), and AppWorld's bound retires at scenario
or supervisor level (\cref{app:external}).

\paragraph{External validity, guards, and comparison.}
Evidence spans pinned GitHub snapshots, a post-cutoff cohort and BIRD. The
six-study compiled-only misses are excerpts departing from identical evidence,
without a counterfactual separating model and context effects. The drift studies
show no verifier protection, and live continuation grading is adverse
(\cref{sec:res-more}); constructed requests support scope admission, not
completeness checking or contract discovery. One adapted-AWM cell does not
establish five-point noninferiority, and no owned backlog records and only one
open PR limit branch coverage (\cref{app:generalization-gate}). Writes,
handoffs, hosted tools, undeclared effects and removed-turn guardrails lie
outside \cref{prop:admission}.

\paragraph{Adaptive comparison and lifecycle cost.}
Development gates on BIRD, AppWorld, and AgentHop~\citep{park2026agenthop}
(\cref{app:adaptive-gates}) show BIRD's one-call candidate trailing a
handwritten wrapper, AppWorld baselines missing competence, and AgentHop
passing competence without a derived dependent selector. None is a tested GAC
advantage; native browser and matched compiler comparisons remain unrun, and a
valid study must freeze candidates and refusals, match continuations, and count
quality, dispatch, fallback, and full cost. Reported savings omit engineering and
maintenance. The registry implements staged promotion, expiry, retirement,
rollback, a kill switch, and manifest checks; the renewal policy is proposed and
unevaluated (\cref{app:lifecycle}).
