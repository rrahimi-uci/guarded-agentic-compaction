\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}

The evidence supports guarded compilation on tested workflows and refusal
when evidence is inadequate.
Across 810 held-out GitHub episodes run under a compiled artifact on the
calibrated model in six studies, one produced a compiled-only contract
failure: 1 of 270 on the excerpt-bearing contracts (one-sided bound 1.7\%) and
0 of 540 on the outcome-only cross-repository contract (that subset covers
354 distinct records) (\cref{app:stats});
\cref{app:limits} pairs limitations with protocols and \cref{app:claims} maps
claims to evidence.

\paragraph{What the certificates cover.} The primary registered certificates are
support-sufficiency statements about a replay-contract event without support
here (\cref{sec:method}): they certify neither graded selectivity of $q$ ---
the cross-repository learned and support-only arms were indistinguishable,
and an amended 184-group issue study retired (\cref{app:prospective}) --- nor the continuation, so the
compiled-only misses lie outside them; the end-to-end certificate is
per-candidate and covers one family. Every bound assumes i.i.d.\ groups:
counting a creation day or an author as one unit retires every primary family
under the registered grid (\cref{tab:effective-units}; single-rule sensitivity
in \cref{app:config}), and AppWorld's bound retires at scenario or supervisor
level (\cref{app:external}).

\paragraph{External validity, guards, and comparison.}
Evidence spans pinned GitHub snapshots, a post-cutoff cohort and BIRD;
model/provider replications are in \cref{sec:res-primary,sec:res-bird}.
The six-study compiled-only misses are excerpts departing from identical
evidence, without a counterfactual separating model and context effects.
Earlier drift studies show no protection: provider-free perturbations are null
by construction, and live continuation grading is adverse (39/60 records wrong
in both arms under the original grader, 40 under its correction;
\cref{app:limits}). Constructed requests support scope admission, not
completeness checking or contract discovery (\cref{sec:res-recovery}).
Fresh adapted AWM comparisons span three workflows and two models, but one
cell does not establish five-point noninferiority; no owned backlog records
and only one open PR limit branch coverage (\cref{app:generalization-gate}).
Native browser and compiler-only causal comparisons remain missing. Manual
authoring is unpriced; the statistical gate adds no observed runtime value on
these candidates. Writes, handoffs, hosted tools, undeclared effects and
removed-turn guardrails lie outside \cref{prop:admission}.
