\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}

The supported claim is narrow: repeated read-only prefixes can become guarded
programs that preserve held-out contracts on the tested GitHub families and
retire when evidence is inadequate. Across 810 held-out episodes run under a compiled artifact on the
calibrated model in six live studies, one produced a compiled-only contract
failure: 1 of 270 on the excerpt-bearing contracts (one-sided bound 1.7\%) and
0 of 540 on the outcome-only cross-repository contract, over 354 distinct
records (\cref{app:stats});
\cref{app:limits} pairs limitations with protocols and \cref{app:claims} maps
claims to evidence.

\paragraph{Three constraints bound the claim.} First, admission is
compiler-wide only when one candidate reaches calibration or the budget is
divided across all that do; PR-outcome and backlog routing calibrated two, where
the corrected bound is 0.057 (\cref{app:config}). Second, with
$|\mathcal{K}|=92$ the bound certifies only full coverage, so no experiment
demonstrates graded selectivity of $q$; prospectively, the learned gate and a
support-only comparator were indistinguishable (\cref{app:prospective}). Third,
the bound assumes i.i.d.\ groups: counting a creation day or an author as one
unit retires every primary family under the registered grid
(\cref{tab:effective-units}; single-rule sensitivity in \cref{app:config}), and
AppWorld's bound retires at scenario or supervisor level (\cref{app:proof}).

\paragraph{External validity, guards, and comparison.}
The richer evidence comes from one pinned snapshot, five frozen
cross-repository snapshots, one post-cutoff cohort, one public benchmark
(BIRD), and one calibrated model family; a second model and a second provider re-derive two of three programs
and refuse the third, and every compiled-only miss is an excerpt departing from
identical evidence, with no counterfactual attributing it to the model rather
than the compiled context (\cref{app:limits}). Under drift we show no
protection: two provider-free perturbation studies cannot fire for these
programs (their oracle is the derived call argument, fixed by the entry id),
the live continuation-graded study is adverse (an emptied list lies inside the
learned hull; both arms answered wrongly on 39 of 60 records), and no held-out
record here shows a guard preventing a wrong compiled answer. We do not price
manual authoring or run workflow-reuse systems head-to-head; a recurrence-only
replay on issue-type routing passed 30/30 at one request per record
(\cref{tab:mechanism-removal}, \cref{app:limits}). The registered
certificates cover an event without support here, so the compiled-only misses
lie outside them; the end-to-end certificate is per-candidate, on one family,
on open or merged records only. Writes, handoffs, hosted tools, undeclared
effects, and removed-turn guardrails lie outside \cref{prop:admission}.
