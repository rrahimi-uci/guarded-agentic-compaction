\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}

Across 810 held-out GitHub episodes run under a compiled artifact on the
calibrated model in six studies, one produced a compiled-only contract
failure: 1 of 270 on the excerpt-bearing contracts (one-sided bound 1.7\%) and
0 of 540 on the outcome-only cross-repository contract (\cref{app:stats});
\cref{app:limits} pairs limitations with protocols and \cref{app:claims} maps
claims to evidence.

\paragraph{What the certificates cover.} The primary certificates
(\cref{tab:certificates}) are support-sufficiency statements: they certify
neither graded selectivity of $q$ (an amended 184-group issue study retired;
\cref{app:prospective}) nor the continuation, so the compiled-only misses lie
outside them; the end-to-end certificate covers one family. Every bound
assumes i.i.d.\ groups:
counting a creation day or an author as one unit retires every primary family
under the registered grid (\cref{tab:effective-units}; single-rule sensitivity
in \cref{app:config}), and AppWorld's bound retires at scenario or supervisor
level (\cref{app:external}).

\paragraph{External validity, guards, and comparison.}
Evidence spans pinned GitHub snapshots, a post-cutoff cohort, and BIRD. The
six-study compiled-only misses are excerpts departing from identical evidence,
without a counterfactual separating model and context effects. Two
provider-free drift studies are null by construction and the live one is
adverse, with both verifier arms wrong on 39 of 60 records
(\cref{app:limits}); the live safeguard study supports scope admission only
(\cref{sec:res-more}). The adapted AWM comparison leaves one
cell inconclusive and, with no owned backlog records and one open PR, thin
branch coverage (\cref{app:generalization-gate}). Manual authoring is
unpriced. Writes, handoffs, hosted tools, undeclared
effects and removed-turn guardrails lie outside \cref{prop:admission}.
