\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}

The supported claim is narrow: repeated read-only prefixes can become guarded
programs that preserve held-out contracts on the tested GitHub families and
retire when evidence is inadequate. No admitted artifact produced a
compiled-only contract failure in any of the 630 compiled held-out episodes
run on its calibrated model across the four live studies. \Cref{app:limits}
expands each limitation; each is paired with a committed protocol.

\paragraph{Three constraints bound the claim.} First, admission control is
compiler-wide only when one candidate reaches calibration or the confidence
budget is divided across all that do. That holds for issue-type routing and for
every cross-repository family; PR-outcome and backlog routing calibrated two
candidates on 92 groups, where the corrected bound is 0.057
(\cref{app:config}). Second, with $|\mathcal{K}|=92$ the registered exact bound
certifies only full coverage, so no experiment here demonstrates graded
selectivity of $q$: the prospective protocol (\cref{app:prospective}) found the
learned gate and a support-only comparator indistinguishable across five
repositories and 240 held-out triples, and $q$ is constant within a repository
there because the entry state is one integer. Third, the binomial result is conditional on i.i.d.\ groups: the
92-record live calibration cohorts span 86--90 distinct creation days and 60--82 distinct authors per
family, so a bound that treats a day or an author as one unit retires in every
family (\cref{tab:effective-units} in \cref{app:limits}), and AppWorld's task
groups share a scenario's supervisor and database (\cref{app:proof} prices the
exposure). The follow-up is to buy calibration groups first: below
$\lceil 92/c\rceil$ of them, no refit of $q$ can be read.

\paragraph{External validity, dispatchability, and comparison.}
The richer evidence comes from one pinned repository snapshot and one
calibrated model family; a same-cohort transfer to a second family kept the
structure and produced the paper's only compiled-only miss (\cref{app:limits}).
Admission is not dispatchability (\cref{eq:prefix}, \cref{sec:res-refusal}). We
show no superiority under drift, do not price manual authoring, and run no
head-to-head against workflow-reuse systems, which make no admissibility
decision to compare against; \cref{tab:mechanism-removal} in \cref{app:limits} prices the guards
internally, and a pre-registered recurrence-only replay on issue-type routing
passed 30/30 with one request per record, so on that cohort the refusal bought
no measured quality (\cref{app:limits}).
Exact contracts also do not certify a
downstream continuation, and writes, handoffs, hosted tools, undeclared effects,
and removed-turn guardrails lie outside \cref{prop:admission}.
