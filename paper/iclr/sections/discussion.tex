\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}

Across 810 compiled held-out GitHub episodes in six studies on the calibrated
model, compiled-only failures are 1/270 on excerpt-bearing contracts
(one-sided bound 1.7\%) and 0/540 on outcome-only contracts (354 distinct
records; \cref{app:stats}). \Cref{app:claims} maps claims to evidence.

\paragraph{Certificate assumptions.} \Cref{tab:evidence-scope,tab:certificates}
separate replay from final-answer risk. The amended 184-group issue study
retired; a sequential extension selects $\eta=0.50$ on 311 pooled groups,
but its adaptive extension and mixed sampling frames leave nominal coverage
unestablished (\cref{app:gate-extension}). Every registered certificate assumes
i.i.d.\ groups:
counting a creation day or an author as one unit retires every primary family
under the registered grid (\cref{tab:effective-units}; single-rule sensitivity
in \cref{app:config}), and AppWorld's bound retires at scenario or supervisor
level (\cref{app:external}).

\paragraph{External validity, guards, and comparison.}
Evidence spans pinned GitHub snapshots, a post-cutoff cohort and BIRD. The six-study compiled-only misses are excerpts departing from identical
evidence, without a counterfactual separating model and context effects.
Earlier drift studies show no protection: provider-free perturbations are null
by construction, and live continuation grading is adverse (39/60 records wrong
in both arms under the original grader, 40 under its correction;
\cref{app:limits}). Constructed requests support scope admission, not
completeness checking or contract discovery (\cref{sec:res-more}).
Fresh adapted AWM comparisons span three workflows and two models, but one
cell does not establish five-point noninferiority; no owned backlog records
and only one open PR limit branch coverage (\cref{app:generalization-gate}).
Writes, handoffs, hosted tools, undeclared effects and removed-turn guardrails
lie outside \cref{prop:admission}.

\paragraph{Remaining comparison and lifecycle cost.}
Native browser and matched compiler comparisons remain missing. A prospective
comparison must freeze candidate families before calibration, retain rejected
families, and compare full admission with simpler compilation using matched
continuations, task errors, wrong dispatches, fallback, and total cost. It is
unrun; identical routing on admitted artifacts cannot answer that question.
The reported deployment savings exclude engineering, review, monitoring, and
recompilation. Snapshot invalidation resets amortization: even a profitable
per-episode prefix may never repay discovery before it expires
(\cref{app:lifecycle}).
