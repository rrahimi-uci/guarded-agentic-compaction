\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}

The supported claim is narrow: repeated read-only prefixes can become guarded
programs that preserve held-out contracts on the tested GitHub families, show
no detected accuracy loss on BIRD, and retire when evidence is inadequate.
Across 810 held-out GitHub episodes run under a compiled artifact on the
calibrated model in six studies, one produced a compiled-only contract
failure: 1 of 270 on the excerpt-bearing contracts (one-sided bound 1.7\%) and
0 of 540 on the outcome-only cross-repository contract (that subset covers
354 distinct records) (\cref{app:stats});
\cref{app:limits} pairs limitations with protocols and \cref{app:claims} maps
claims to evidence.

\paragraph{What the certificates cover.} The primary registered certificates are
support-sufficiency statements about a replay-contract event without support
here (\cref{sec:method}): they certify neither graded selectivity of $q$ ---
prospectively, the learned gate and a support-only comparator were
indistinguishable (\cref{app:prospective}) --- nor the continuation, so the
compiled-only misses lie outside them; the end-to-end certificate is
per-candidate and covers one family. Every bound assumes i.i.d.\ groups:
counting a creation day or an author as one unit retires every primary family
under the registered grid (\cref{tab:effective-units}; single-rule sensitivity
in \cref{app:config}), and AppWorld's bound retires at scenario or supervisor
level (\cref{app:external}).

\paragraph{External validity, guards, and comparison.}
The richer evidence comes from one pinned snapshot, five frozen
cross-repository snapshots, one post-cutoff cohort, one public benchmark
(BIRD), and one calibrated model family (replications in
\cref{sec:res-primary,sec:res-bird}); every GitHub compiled-only miss is an excerpt departing
from identical evidence, with no counterfactual attributing it to the model
rather than the compiled context (\cref{app:limits}). Under drift we show no
protection: two provider-free perturbation studies are null by construction
(the simulated tools stay total; on the GitHub records the oracle is the
derived call argument, fixed by the entry id),
the live continuation-graded study is adverse (an emptied list lies inside the
learned hull; both arms answered wrongly on 39 of 60 records under the original grader,
40 under its later correction), and no held-out
record here shows a guard preventing a wrong compiled answer (\cref{app:limits}). We do not price
manual authoring or run workflow-reuse systems head-to-head; a recurrence-only
replay on issue-type routing passed 30/30 at one request per record
(\cref{tab:recurrence-only}). Writes, handoffs, hosted tools, undeclared
effects, and removed-turn guardrails lie outside \cref{prop:admission}.
