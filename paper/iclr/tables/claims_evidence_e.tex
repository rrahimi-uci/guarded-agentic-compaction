Drift ablation (provider-free, demonstration substrate) &
Two arms (induced versus permissive verifier) on an identical program and identical sealed-test windows; six artifacts, three demonstrations, 144 paired groups; zero wrong outcomes in both arms (upper bound 0.0206 per arm, no discordant pair); guarded abstention on invariant perturbations 0.2477 pooled, 0.3333 on \code{permissioned\_rag}. &
\path{paper/results/drift_ablation/results.json} (\code{pooled}, \code{decision}); \path{paper/scripts/drift_ablation_study.py}; \path{paper/supplementary/drift-robustness-ablation-protocol.md}. &
The pre-declared null: the suite does not separate the arms on simulated total tools. Not equivalence; no claim about real records, the GitHub families, cost, or production safety. The manual arm was not run. \\
\addlinespace[2pt]
Recorded-replay drift ablation (provider-free, 90 primary records) &
Three arms (induced verifier; permissive verifier; hand-written program with permissive verifier) on identical reconstructed windows; 89 paired records; zero wrong outcomes in every arm (upper bound 0.0331; Holm-adjusted McNemar $p=1$ for both comparisons); guarded abstention on invariant perturbations 0.1049 pooled; every transform changes at least one recorded call per family. &
\path{paper/results/drift_recorded_replay/results.json} (\code{pooled}, \code{families.*.\allowbreak applicability}); \path{paper/scripts/drift_recorded_replay_study.py}; \path{paper/supplementary/drift-recorded-replay-protocol.md}. &
The null, explained: the oracle compares derived call arguments, every admitted program's only derived argument is the record identifier (bound at entry, re-read from the first tool's echo), and no transform in the suite alters it, so \emph{wrong} is unreachable under this suite. Program-output endpoint only; no continuation grading; not a fresh cohort; not equivalence. \\
\addlinespace[2pt]
Time-forward cohort; end-to-end certificate (PR-outcome) &
1{,}095 post-cutoff records acquired; 811 fresh PRs; splits 60/132/132 frozen first. Re-derived artifact (one frozen candidate) dispatched on 132/132 fresh calibration groups with 0 end-to-end contract misses: single-rule bound 0.0173 at 90\% confidence, admits. Evaluation on 60 fresh records: 60/60 in all four conditions; re-derived artifact $-75.0\%$ requests, $-82.7\%$ tokens; retained artifact abstains 60/60 under its old pin. &
\path{paper/results/time_forward/pr_outcome/certificate.json}, \path{.../evaluation.json}, \path{.../selection.json}; \path{paper/scripts/time_forward_pr_outcome_study.py}; \path{paper/supplementary/time-forward-end-to-end-protocol.md}. &
Per-candidate, one family, i.i.d.\ groups from one repository's post-cutoff records; issue-type and backlog are NO-GO on class availability. Preservation at full coverage, not superiority; the hand-written program ties. \\
\addlinespace[2pt]
Continuation-graded drift ablation (live, 60 primary records) &
1{,}080 cells; the guarded arm answers silently wrong on 39 of 60 records as graded (40 counting one grader substring artifact, PR 6694), all under emptied lists; the permissive arm on the same records (no discordant record). Out-of-hull corruption falls back in both arms before any continuation (interpreter on renamed keys; verifier clauses in the guarded arm, typed projection in the permissive arm on nulls and error payloads); duplicated and padded lists cost the guarded arm 11--14 abstentions per 30, one of which itself missed. &
\path{paper/results/drift_continuation_graded/results.json} (\code{per\_cell}, \code{decision}); \path{paper/scripts/drift_continuation_graded_study.py}; \path{paper/supplementary/drift-continuation-graded-protocol.md}. &
The pre-declared adverse branch: on these records the induced verifier prevented no silent wrong answer. A contract induced from traces cannot flag corruption that lands on a legitimate value. Not a production-safety statement; the calibrated model only. \\
\addlinespace[2pt]
BIRD: a live SQL agent on a public benchmark (five databases, two designs) &
1{,}316 discovery episodes, zero failures. Standard design (table choice per question): all five families retire, four at synthesis (\code{ungroundable\_slot}) and one on support (34 of 92). Schema-first design: all five admit \code{list\_tables} $\to$ \code{get\_schema}(all tables), $m=1$, 92/0 gates, clean strict audit. Held out (150 questions): dispatch 150/150; correct 97 compiled, 94 baseline, 94 repeat, 94 hand-written; against the warm repeat $-44.9\%$ requests, $-9.8\%$ tokens, $-29.4\%$ latency, $-6.3\%$ cost. &
\path{paper/results/bird/summary.json}, \path{.../<design>/<database>/compile.json}, \path{.../evaluation.json}; \path{paper/scripts/bird_sql_agent_study.py}; \path{paper/supplementary/bird-sql-agent-protocol.md}. &
One model, one benchmark split, 30 held-out questions per database. Condition order was not rotated as registered, so cost and latency are reported against the warm repeat run. No detected accuracy loss (McNemar $p=0.45$); not superiority. The hand-written prefetch matches the compiled program. \\
