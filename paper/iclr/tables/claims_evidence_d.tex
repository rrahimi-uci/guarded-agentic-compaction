Recorded-replay drift ablation (provider-free, 90 primary records) &
Three arms (induced verifier; permissive verifier; hand-written program with permissive verifier) on identical reconstructed windows; 89 paired records; zero wrong outcomes in every arm (upper bound 0.0331; Holm-adjusted McNemar $p=1$ for both comparisons); guarded abstention on invariant perturbations 0.1049 pooled; every transform changes at least one recorded call per family. &
\path{paper/results/drift_recorded_replay/results.json} (\code{pooled}, \code{families.*.applicability}); \path{paper/scripts/drift_recorded_replay_study.py}; \path{paper/supplementary/drift-recorded-replay-protocol.md}. &
The null, explained: the oracle compares derived call arguments and every admitted program derives its arguments from the entry record alone, so \emph{wrong} is unreachable under this suite. Program-output endpoint only; no continuation grading; not a fresh cohort; not equivalence. \\

Out-of-hull corruption is refused by the typed projection in both arms; duplicated and padded lists cost the guarded arm 11--14 abstentions per 30 with no benefit. &
