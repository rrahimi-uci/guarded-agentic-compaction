The hand-written program is the oracle throughout: a person who knows the workflow can write it, and the question is whether the compiler finds it without that knowledge.
\Cref{tab:compiler-decisions} shows that it recovers the program a person would write wherever the retained traces support one, and refuses, with a stated stage and reason, wherever they do not.
Savings are scoped to a compatible snapshot: every artifact pins its snapshot and abstains on any other, so a changed snapshot is recompiled rather than reused.
