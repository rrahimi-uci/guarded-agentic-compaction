Response to the automated pre-submission feedback (PAT, 12 September 2026)

The PAT comments were written on the September 24 draft. We treated them as a checklist, not as a review, and audited every item against the revised PDF; the ledger with one row per item is in the repository (paper/iclr/notes/pat_feedback_disposition.md). Section and appendix numbers below refer to the revised PDF.

Weaknesses. (1) Comparisons with other compilers: an adapted offline Agent Workflow Memory comparison was run (8,172 episodes on 1,362 fresh records; Table 3, Appendix K); Appendix G states, per system, why AWM, LLMCompiler, plan caching, EvoC2F and Agent JIT cannot be dropped into an exact-contract, admit-or-retire evaluation; Appendix N compares design properties (Table 46) and reports a same-IR four-policy funnel over all 13 retained cohorts plus a sealed live pilot on 193 fresh issues (Tables 47 and 48). No external compiler has a runnable artifact, and we make no ranking claim. (2) Proposition 1 versus the two-candidate primary families: Section 3 states, directly after the proposition, that the multi-candidate search does not inherit the certificate without candidate freezing; Section 5.4 and Table 1 report the per-candidate bound 0.0498, the two-candidate adjusted bound 0.0569 and the 106-group requirement (Appendix A, Table on multiplicity accounting). Corollary 2 covers the frozen search, and the post-cutoff and multi-repository studies freeze one candidate before calibration. (3) Position invariant: the read-only prologue relaxation was implemented and measured on the 8,190 released AppWorld trajectories; it recovers 2 of 2,340 ReAct runs because those agents seldom execute the admitted region (Section 5.4, Appendix F.4). Sections 1 and 2 now say plainly that the system is an initial read-only prefix compiler. (4) Step-gate arithmetic: Section 3 derives that abstaining on one of 92 groups gives U = 0.0503 > 0.05 and that certifying coverage c needs at least ceil(92/c) groups (184 for 50%); Appendices C and H say the pre-registered three-level criterion was unattainable at that cohort size and that the gate behaves as a support-sufficiency floor. (5) Live-provider specification: Section 4 names gpt-5.6-luna through the openai-agents Responses interface at reasoning effort low; Appendix C gives the settings, SDK versions, timeouts, concurrency and the per-token rate card with retrieval dates, and discloses that only model aliases, not immutable checkpoints, are retained.

Formulation and pipeline. Eq. (4) now takes the group measure on both sides and the loss is defined per certificate type; the primary certificates label replay-contract violations only, so we did not relabel them as end-to-end (that would have been inaccurate), and the end-to-end studies are named as such. Eq. (1) is single-source, matching Algorithm 3. FitScore is specified (balanced logistic regression, seven features, standardization, label construction). The 23-operator library is tabulated (Appendix C). Eq. (6) has a pre-synthesis form with rho = 1 and a realized form. Algorithm 4 checks position explicitly, declares its runtime context, and quota_attested is defined; Algorithm 3 separates slot names from values and orders resource edges.

Experiments. Resource figures for the hand-written comparator are tabulated (Appendix D); the 34/0/0 family replay split and the 11/11 admission test split are distinguished (Table 4 caption, Appendix F.4); pytorch/pytorch retires at admission with no group accepted at any threshold, with the 84/30/2 class composition reported and the cause stated as not established; the gate-frontier counts are reported per arm under intention to treat (240/240, 240/240, 239/240).

Not applied, with reasons. The abstract "artifacts" are line-end hyphenation dropped by text extraction, not misspellings. The suggestion to describe the gate as certifying end-to-end compliance was not adopted for the primary families, whose labels are replay-only. The 239 figure applies to the support-only arm alone.

Remaining. Two PAT requests are not fully met in this revision: a main-text statement that the primary bounds are per-candidate appears in Section 5.4 and the Table 1 caption but not in the abstract (page budget), and mitigations for non-exchangeable groups are discussed in Appendix G rather than Section 7. Both are recorded as open in the ledger.
