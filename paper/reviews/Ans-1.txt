6. Lifecycle policy (your W6 and Q4). Paraphrase: what evidence triggers recompilation or retirement, and what calibration is required before reactivation? Distinguish implemented policy from future work.

Answer:
- Implemented: rejection of any incompatible manifest (a change to a pinned model, prompt, tool, policy, effect catalog or snapshot), preset expiry, explicit retirement, rollback, a kill switch and staged promotion.
- Not implemented: no trigger reacts to accumulating failures or to a distribution change that passes existing guards. We say so.
- Proposed, not evaluated: suspend on a confirmed contract violation or an invalidated pin. Audit outcomes and fallback rates against a prospectively specified rule. Reactivate only as a new version with a declared population, a frozen candidate and loss, and fresh disjoint calibration that includes failed baseline runs.
