Response to Reviewer 1

Thank you for the careful review. Your soundness points were correct. Numbers refer to the revised PDF.

W3 and Q2 (loss includes downstream errors, labels measure replay agreement; which 92-group labels include answer errors?). None of them, and the reviewed loss and labels did not match. The labels come from grc/compile.py::_calibration_samples, which replays the frozen program over recorded tool results and compares outputs after the verifier accepts; the continuation never runs. So 0/92 (U = 0.0498) is a replay bound for one fixed candidate on the filtered population. Section 2 defines the loss per certificate; Table 28 names the event behind each. Two new studies grade final answers after the continuation: post-cutoff PR-outcome, one frozen candidate, 0 violations in 132 fresh pull requests, 90% bound 0.0173 (Section 5.4); and the multi-repository study below.

W4 and Q3 (outcome-filtered calibration population; could excluded records pass the guard?). Yes. Appendix G accounts for all 396 discovery records (2, 1 and 0 excluded as inexact per family; 14, 15 and 16 eligible records unused after the 16/8/92 split). The three excluded records pass the saved guard and gate, so we withdraw the claim that the primary bounds cover the unfiltered population. The repair you suggest was run for PR-outcome (Appendix F.2, Table 19): one pull request per author, sampled before any outcome filter, every eligible group labelled, failures included, one candidate frozen per repository. Registered stage: four repositories all admitted, 3 violations in 760 groups (pooled U = 0.0133); Amendment 3, registered after those results were seen, added seven at n = 400: five admitted, node and cpython retired. Over eleven repositories the compiled, unchanged and hand-written arms meet 3,510, 3,507 and 3,504 of 3,560 contracts. Adverse: on godot the compiled arm trails the unchanged agent, 393 vs 399 (McNemar p = 0.070).

W2a (no comparison with EvoC2F or Agent JIT). No external compiler could be run: EvoC2F has no inspectable implementation (HTTP 401) and Agent JIT plans web tasks. We ran the matched comparison inside our pipeline instead (new Appendix N subsection): the shipped compiler four times over all 13 retained cohorts with statistical admission on/off and grounding on/off. Admission never changed a decision; grounding changed one (the issue-type third read, refused for an unwitnessed literal; replayed, 0 violations in 92 groups). A sealed pilot on 193 fresh issues (GAC recompiled from 128 fresh traces; three arms paired on 65) found the refusal did not recur, and the GAC, unguarded-replay and unchanged arms met the contract on 65/65, 63/65 and 64/65 at 1, 1 and 4 requests. No measured quality benefit of the guards on this workload, and no cost. Also new: an adapted Agent Workflow Memory comparison, 8,172 episodes on 1,362 fresh records, noninferior at a five-point margin in 5 of 6 cells at 36.9 to 79.1% lower cost (Table 3), and a design comparison (Table 46): none of the cited systems states a finite-sample bound after admission. We make no ranking claim against them.

W2b (reviewed Table 2 hard to interpret). It reports trace benchmarks on which no model runs, so it shows only whether compilation is feasible or refused; now Table 4 with a caption key. Quality where a live agent runs: GitHub 90/90 vs 89/90 (Table 2); BIRD 97 vs 94 on 150 sealed questions with 44.9% fewer requests, trailing on a second model and the training split (Section 5.2); end-to-end 3,510 vs 3,507.

W1 (introduction; issue #4420 without context). Agreed. Section 1 now defines the triage task before the example and says #4420 is one of 30 held-out records; the abstract and Section 1 are rewritten around automating the program an engineer would write.

W5 and Q4 (lifecycle policy). Implemented: manifest checks at dispatch, expiry, retirement, rollback, kill switch, staged promotion. Not implemented: any failure-rate trigger or sequential guarantee. Proposed, not evaluated: suspension on a confirmed violation or invalidated pin; reactivation only after fresh disjoint calibration. Measured case: the retained PR-outcome artifact abstained on all 60 fresh pull requests of a new snapshot; a re-derived version was admitted after calibration on 132 groups. Appendix D separates implemented from proposed and cites SKILL.nb.

Q1 (references). Section 4 cites each benchmark with its release; Table 16 specifies the GitHub tasks; Appendix F.4 maps each external benchmark to its use .

Q5 (content-addressed manifest). The record of prompt, model, tools, policies and effect catalog, each by version or digest; dispatch requires equality on every pinned field (Eq. 3), so a certificate does not transfer across a changed pin. The 60/60 abstentions above are this rule working.

We are glad to answer anything further.
