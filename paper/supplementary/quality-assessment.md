only checked-in, reproducible evidence; proposed and preflight-only experiments receive no
empirical credit.

## Verdicts

- **Artifact/manuscript engineering: 95/100.** The artifact is organized, auditable,
  reproducible, and publication quality as a research prototype.
- **Top-tier main-track readiness: 84/100 (major revision).** The expanded paid
  replication repairs the earlier design flaw, passes 30/30 under safe partial compilation,
  and shows the macro is the stronger default. One domain, a 9.5% zero-event upper bound,
  no learned-workflow head-to-head, and an all-or-none gate prevent a broad claim.

The 90/100 target is met for artifact quality, not scientific acceptance readiness. A
defensible 90+ main-track score requires a powered multi-domain non-inferiority study, a
non-degenerate post-selection risk--coverage frontier, prospective continuation admission,
and at least one same-task learned-workflow comparator. GEPA is relevant when residual
prompt evolution is evaluated; it is not a substitute for the macro/compiler baseline.
