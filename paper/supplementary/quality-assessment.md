This rubric separates artifact quality from scientific acceptance readiness. Scores use
only checked-in, reproducible evidence; proposed and preflight-only experiments receive no
empirical credit.

## Verdicts

- **Artifact/manuscript engineering: 97/100.** The artifact is organized, auditable,
  reproducible, and publication quality as a research prototype.
- **Top-tier main-track readiness: 84/100 (major revision).** The expanded paid
  replication and prospective portfolio pilot now implement and test reviewed action
  selection: macro is chosen from 30 groups and passes 12/12 fresh contracts while reducing
  every measured resource. GCS additionally matches the measured macro on 12/12 disjoint
  fresh cases and reduces provider work. A new six-case study gives an independent manual
  program the same pre-model position and finds structural parity; official GEPA is also
  executed but retains its seed. The ten-benchmark extension establishes interoperability
  breadth and replicates refusal on API-Bank, but one paired real-record domain, no
  action-diverse families, no executable workflow-compiler comparator, and an all-or-none
  compiler gate prevent a broad claim.

The 90/100 target is met for artifact quality and manuscript presentation, not scientific
acceptance readiness. A
defensible stronger main-track score requires a powered multi-domain non-inferiority study, a
non-degenerate post-selection risk--coverage frontier, prospective continuation admission,
multiple workflow families with different optimal actions, and at least one executable
same-task workflow-compiler comparator. The manual pre-model and GEPA conditions are now
implemented; GEPA remains complementary rather than a substitute for the compiler baseline.

The implemented vulnerability/HMDA preflight and sealed multidomain control plane improve
future-study feasibility but do not change the score: SEC, human approvals, and all live
conditions remain unrun, so no additional experimental credit is assigned.
