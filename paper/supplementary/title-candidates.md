## Selected (2026-08-03)

> **When Traces Are Not Enough:**  
> **Guarded Compilation of Tool-Using Agents**

This title states the paper's central negative and constructive message: recurrence in
historical traces does not justify replacement, so compilation requires provenance,
effect barriers, runtime guards, and evidence-based admission. It avoids claiming that
reasoning is preserved, which the current quality oracle does not establish, and avoids
using “specialization,” the name of a broader pass excluded from the headline study.
The natural-order exact-source follow-up strengthens this rationale: it records one
compiler-only factual miss, so a preservation title would now contradict the evidence.

## Other candidates considered

2. **From Recurrent Traces to Guarded Programs: Selective Compilation for Tool-Using

4. **Beyond Workflow Recurrence: Evidence-Gated Compilation of Agent Traces** — strong

5. **Guarded Agentic Compaction: Provenance-Constrained Compilation of Repeated Tool
   Workflows** — searchable and precise, but reads more like a mechanism label than a
   scientific claim.

The method name remains *guarded agentic compaction* (GAC); the paper title names the
research finding rather than forcing the method acronym into the headline.
