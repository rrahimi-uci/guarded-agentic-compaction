\textbf{Problem.}  A mature tool-using agent spends model calls reselecting the same
read-only evidence path.  Recurrence alone does not license removing them: an argument may
not be grounded in observable state, an effect may not permit speculation, and a
replacement may not hold on the next input.

\textbf{Gap.}  Prior systems tune prompts, reuse workflows, or schedule calls.  The open
question is narrower --- what evidence licenses replacing a recurrent region, and when must
an optimizer refuse?

\textbf{Method.}  \emph{Guarded agentic compaction} (\method) reconstructs typed
value-level provenance, treats unknown effects, writes, approvals, handoffs, and
unsupported dispatch positions as hard barriers, and synthesizes programs from a closed
23-operator library.  Induced replay contracts gate execution, and a threshold from a
pre-specified finite grid carries an exact-binomial bound on \emph{registered-contract}
violations.  Refusal is the default output: the compiler itself remains binary --- emit a
guarded artifact, or retain the unchanged agent.  A portfolio layer then compares only
measured actions and otherwise returns the baseline.

\textbf{Results.}  Under a task-level prompt naming neither tools nor order, 130 of 132
real GitHub traces follow one three-read path --- yet the third argument is not groundable,
so \method abstains from the full region and emits a two-read prefix.  That partial
artifact holds 30/30 task contracts while cutting requests 50.0\% and estimated cost
32.0\%.  A hand-written composite tool matches its request saving and beats it on tokens
and dollars.  In a post-study extension, guarded composite synthesis packages the
three-read program behind a verified pre-model projection.  On 12 fresh exploratory
pairs it matches the measured macro's 12/12 exact quality while using 50.0\% fewer
provider requests, 38.9\% fewer tokens, and 32.3\% lower estimated cost.  A subsequent
six-record, five-condition study gives a hand-authored pre-model program the same
opportunity: it and GCS both pass 6/6 and tie at one request, one exposed interface, and
identical input tokens.  Official GEPA 0.1.4 retains its seed after 14 task evaluations
and leaves deployment requests unchanged; its 59-request optimization overhead is
reported separately.  A deeper three-read artifact saves 75.0\% of requests but loses a factual
contract (17/18).  On NESTFUL, provenance places the true producer among candidates for
96.3\% of 5,746 slots yet resolves only 80.7\% uniquely, and every recurrent family retires
at the gate.

\textbf{Contribution.}  Traces establish recurrence, not admissibility.  We specify the
evidence a compiler needs and the barriers it must respect, and report four negative
results: efficiency gains establish neither factual quality, nor superiority over
hand-written code, nor prompt-optimizer improvement, nor portfolio superiority, nor
admission safety.
