Two different axes

Compaction removes the call. Compression shrinks it.

Both cut tokens, by mechanisms that do not overlap — which is why they compose, and why only one of them can touch the request count.

The honest caveat
Our numbers compare against an uncompressed baseline.
So the token reduction should be read as the part attributable to removing model boundaries. The request reduction is not exposed to that caveat — compression cannot remove a call.
