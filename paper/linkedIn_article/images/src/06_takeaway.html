Practical takeaway

Optimize only the boundaries that evidence can license

The compiler is conservative by construction. Every claimed speedup is conditional on evidence that survived a stage designed to reject it.

What stays

Keeps

The model still handles every step that needs judgment, adaptation, or fresh reasoning.

  • novel or one-off requests
  • anything past the read-only prefix
What shifts

Changes

Deterministic code replaces repeated read-only prefixes — only when the evidence is strong.

  • fewer provider round-trips
  • same exact task outcome
What stops

Refuses

Unsafe effects, missing provenance, manifest drift, and thin support never ship.

  • writes, approvals, handoffs
  • families below the risk bound
Traces establish recurrence, not admissibility.
That is why the method optimizes only the part of the workflow evidence can license.
