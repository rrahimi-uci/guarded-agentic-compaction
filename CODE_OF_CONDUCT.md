# Code of conduct

We want `guarded-agentic-compaction` to be a rigorous, welcoming research and engineering
community. Be respectful, assume good intent, critique claims with evidence, and make room
for participants with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, unwanted sexual attention, disclosure of private
information, or sustained disruption are not acceptable in project spaces. Maintainers may
edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when needed to protect the
community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Reports will be
handled as confidentially and promptly as practical. Good-faith disagreement about
methods, evidence, and safety is encouraged; attacks on people are not.
